The cards

Your card shows your rank

Every member gets a Guild Card with a photo, stamps, stats and a LinkedIn link. Turn up, earn stamps and level up. A beginner holds a clean, plain card. The higher the tier, the more colour, metal and foil it carries.

Rank up

  1. D-TierInitiatesNew or rarely at events. Clean and plain.
  2. C-TierGuild RegularsTurns up often. Full colour, simple frame.
  3. B-TierOperators & StrategistsSteady at events and contributing. Metal finish.
  4. A-TierElite Captains & HeavyweightsLeads, mentors and shows up. Gold and foil.
  5. S-TierGod-Tier / UnmatchedExceptionally rare. Panel-approved. Full holo.

Sample cards, shown as a concept for the app. Names, stats and numbers are examples. Stats are self-reported: the Guild does not check trading results and does not give financial advice.

Stamps

Earned in person. Go to an event, run a session, mentor someone or win a bracket and an organiser scans you in. Stamps fill the card and level it up.

Links

Connections that are real. A link forms only when two members both confirm they met at an event. Add your LinkedIn if you want it on the card. It is optional and never public by default.

Rare cards

Foil and numbered versions go to flagship events and top contributors. Rank is earned by showing up, never bought, and nobody opens random packs.
